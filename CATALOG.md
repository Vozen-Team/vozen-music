# Vozen Music catalog

175 recordings across seven stations. Source and objective audio review: 2026-10-27.

Source license and instrumental evidence: [catalog-sources.json](licenses/catalog-sources.json). Objective levels, gain, sample rates, and file hashes: [audio-quality.json](licenses/audio-quality.json).

Eight lower-quality/anomalous recordings were replaced. Long near-silent tails were shortened. Source-based instrumental classifications remain separate from audio-quality measurements: there has been no independent full listening or vocal audit. Genre placement is editorial; some entries are short loops/cues.
