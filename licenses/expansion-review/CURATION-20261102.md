## Evidence and selection

`curation-20261102.json` records 147 source-page CC0 declarations, their URLs, authors, page hashes and source-file links. The local source snapshots are in `output/six-stations-research/`. These declarations are evidence of what the source publishes, not an independent investigation of the rights chain. Only pages with a CC0 licence link in the actual licence field were admitted as source leads; prose mentioning CC0 does not override another licence. Source authors are extracted from the author field, not from commenters.

129 initial local candidates were prepared. Eighteen were excluded from the listening pool because their archive explicitly identified another genre or because they were short-form loops below the 30-second curation threshold. Source format copies, prior versions, known vocal/radio edits and known existing audio are not used to fill quotas. A source using sample-library material was held for separate rights review. The Itch page for `more ice, please?` was not admitted: it combines a CC0 label with an additional no-resale statement. A Free Music Archive lead could not be fully checked (403).

The retained 111 candidates total 276.49 minutes. They passed full FFmpeg decoding, sample rate >=44.1 kHz, PCM-hash uniqueness against each other and the active catalog, loudness/true-peak measurement and gain calculation capped to a projected peak <=-2 dBFS. Silence events are retained for review. This does not prove subjective quality, absence of voice, independent compositions or runtime behaviour in Discord.

Twenty-five source Vorbis copies had non-monotonic timestamps and failed the strict full-decode check. Originals were preserved. Separate listening copies were re-encoded with `ffmpeg -map 0:a:0 -af asetpts=N/SR/TB -c:a libvorbis -q:a 6` and then passed the same full checks. Per-file original hashes and transformations are retained. Do not download the original and present it as the repaired derivative.
